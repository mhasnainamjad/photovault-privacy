PhotoVault — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

The short version

PhotoVault does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, photos, videos, or files. This is not a policy of limited data use — the app has no server and requests no network permission from the operating system, so it has no way to send your data anywhere even if it wanted to.

You can verify this yourself: open your device's Settings → Apps → PhotoVault → Permissions. There is no network or internet permission listed, because the app never requests one.

What the app actually does

  • Encrypts the photos, videos, and files you choose to import, using a key derived from a password only you know.
  • Stores that encrypted data locally on your device only.
  • Never creates an account, never asks for your email, and has no password-reset process — a one-time recovery phrase shown when you set up the app is the only backup, and PhotoVault never stores or sees a copy of it.

In-app purchases

PhotoVault offers optional one-time purchases through Google Play's billing system. These purchases are processed entirely by Google Play; PhotoVault never receives or stores your payment information. Google's handling of that transaction is governed by Google Play's own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Given the app's architecture — no server, no accounts, no data collection — no future change to this policy would involve your data being collected where it previously wasn't.
